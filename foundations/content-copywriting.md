# Content & copywriting (after competitor analysis)

Design and content are one decision: layout is planned around real messages. Write content **after** research (`workflows/research-pipeline.md`), because the copy must answer the objections and gaps you found. [Expert unless tagged]

## 1. Rules
- **No invented facts:** no made-up stats, testimonials, clients, awards, years in business or guarantees. Missing facts become `PLACEHOLDER: <what's needed>`. [Source: DOCX]
- **People-first:** write for the visitor's decision, not for keywords. Google ranks helpful, original content; keyword stuffing hurts.
- **Specific beats clever:** "Pickup in 24 hours across Islamabad" beats "Revolutionizing logistics".
- **Banned filler:** "Unlock the power of", "seamless", "cutting-edge", "revolutionize", "elevate your", "in today's fast-paced world", "game-changer", lorem ipsum. [Source: anti-slop]
- **Ask for the brand voice** (or derive it from existing content) and keep it consistent.

## 2. Messaging hierarchy (build before writing sections)
```
Audience:          who, in their words
Problem:           the pain/job, in their words (from review mining)
Promise (H1):      the outcome we deliver
Proof:             why believe it (real evidence)
Differentiator:    the gap competitors leave open
Objections → answers: 3–5 pairs
Primary CTA:       verb + outcome
```

## 3. Section copy formulas
| Section | Formula | Example shape |
|---|---|---|
| Hero H1 | Outcome + for whom (+ how) | "Payroll for small teams, done in 5 minutes" |
| Hero subhead | How it works or key proof, 1–2 lines | "Run payroll, taxes and payslips from one screen. Trusted by 2,000 shops." (only if true) |
| Feature | Benefit headline + feature detail | "Never miss a deadline: automatic tax filing on the 15th" |
| How it works | Step verb + effort-reducing detail | "1. Connect your bank (2 min)" |
| Testimonial | Specific result + name, role, company | "Cut our close from 5 days to 1." Name, CFO, Company |
| FAQ | Real question, direct first sentence, then detail | Q in customer words; A starts with yes/no/number |
| CTA block | Outcome restated + action + anxiety remover | "Start your free trial. No card needed." |

Headline helpers: **PAS** (Problem → Agitate → Solve), **AIDA** (Attention → Interest → Desire → Action), **Before–After–Bridge**. Use them as thinking tools, not templates.

## 4. SEO-aware writing
- One topic per page, matched to search intent (seo.md).
- Primary keyword naturally in: title, H1 (or close variant), first 100 words, one H2, URL, image alt where it fits.
- Cover the subtopics top results cover, then add what they miss (experience, data, examples, local detail).
- Answer-first paragraphs: start with the direct answer, then explain. This helps readers and search features. No special "AI formatting" is needed (Google, May 2026).
- Internal links with descriptive anchors to related services, products and guides.
- FAQs for real user questions (FAQ rich results no longer show in Google, but FAQs still help users and conversions).

## 5. Readability
- Plain language; aim for roughly grade 7–9 reading level for consumer sites (adjust for expert audiences).
- Sentences under ~20 words on average; paragraphs 1–3 sentences on the web.
- Front-load key words in headings and links (people scan the first words).
- Microcopy for buttons, errors, empty states and confirmations: say what happened and what to do next.

## 6. Localization
- Write for the market: currency, units, date formats, local proof, local terms.
- For Urdu/Arabic and other RTL languages, support RTL layout (`dir="rtl"`, logical CSS properties) and fonts with proper script support (typography.md §5).
- Use hreflang for language/region versions (seo.md).

## 7. Content deliverable format
For each page, deliver: messaging hierarchy · page copy by section · title tag + meta description · H1/H2 outline · image alt texts · internal link suggestions · schema type · placeholders list.
